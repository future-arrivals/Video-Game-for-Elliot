Pick a look for the Pumpkin Patch

Three ways the world around the characters could look. Each one shows walking around (left) and a battle (right). Point at the big letter you like best.

A

Paper Page

The whole world is drawn on the same white printer paper as his characters, with the same black marker. One simple rule keeps it calm: things you can touch, fight or use are drawn in thick black marker with full colour; scenery is drawn in light grey pencil with pale colour. His characters look like they were always part of the page.

B

Pop-Up Paper

A pop-up book made of construction paper: flat coloured paper layers glued down for the ground, hills and sky. Anything you can use stands up out of the page with a white cut edge and a shadow, like a paper puppet. Folding and unfolding fits right in: the characters are paper too.

C

Lantern Night

Halloween night in deep blue. The world is soft and dim, and only the things that matter are lit up: his characters, candy, and the places where a power can be used. Very easy to read on a TV across the room, and spooky-cosy rather than scary.

Mockup notes: characters are stand-ins made from photos of the drawings (rough cut-outs; the clean SVG redraws will replace them). The ice cream (A) and fish (C) are quick vector redraws and the monster (B) is a posterised version, to show how clean flat characters sit in each world. Faint circles bottom-left and bottom-right are the touch-control fallback, hidden when a controller is connected. Coloured shapes on move buttons are the matching PlayStation buttons (cross, circle, triangle); stars under a move show how many hits it does.
